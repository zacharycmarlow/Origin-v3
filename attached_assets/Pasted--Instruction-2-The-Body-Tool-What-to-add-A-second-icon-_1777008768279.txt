# Instruction 2: The Body Tool

## What to add

A second icon joins the toolbar: a small human figure silhouette, teal (`#2E5D4B`), 28px. Sits above the Stream icon. Tapping it opens a slide-in panel from the right edge of the screen.

## The Panel

Covers approximately 40% of screen width on desktop, 85% on mobile. The step content behind dims with a subtle scrim. Tap the scrim or a close X top-right to dismiss. Title at top: "The Body" in the display font, teal.

## The Body Diagram

A simple, elegant, gender-neutral human figure facing forward. Line art, matching the app's aesthetic. On this figure, 12 tappable energy points shown as small dots:

- Crown (top of head)
- Third Eye (forehead)
- Throat (neck)
- Heart (center chest)
- Solar Plexus (upper abdomen)
- Sacral (lower abdomen)
- Root (base of spine)
- Jaw
- Left Shoulder
- Right Shoulder
- Belly (lower gut, distinct from sacral)
- Hands (both, one point)

Idle state: small muted dots. Points with existing notes: soft glowing halo, brighter with more notes.

## When the user taps a point

A small card appears near the tapped point (positioned to stay on screen):

- Energy center name (e.g., "Heart")
- Text input: "What do you feel here?" with the existing voice input mic
- Below: any previous notes at this point, listed with a colored chapter tag and timestamp
- A "Save" button

## Chapter color coding for note tags

- I Prologue: warm gold
- II Wound: deep red
- III Gift: amber
- IV Source: deep blue
- V Narrator: silver
- VI Dream: violet
- VII Ending: teal

## On save

The note persists in the `bodyEntries` array in UserStore:
```json
{
  "id": "body-[uuid]",
  "energyCenter": "heart",
  "chapter": "[current chapter id]",
  "note": "user text",
  "timestamp": "ISO datetime"
}
```

The point's halo updates. The card can be dismissed.

## Body prompt hint

Some somatic steps in the chapter data should be able to trigger a gentle pulse on the Body toolbar icon. Add support for a `bodyPrompt: true` flag on step definitions. When the step engine encounters this flag, pulse the Body icon for 5 seconds. Do not add the flag to any steps yet — just support the behavior so it can be activated later when chapter content is updated.
