## NOTES ON WHAT THESE ADDITIONS DO

The outward orientation enters through the material, not through announcement. No one is told "this is also a personal branding exercise." The prompts simply ask questions that produce outward-facing material:

- "What has it cost the people around them?" produces accountability and relational awareness
- "Who else lives in this territory?" produces audience identification
- "Whose life changes because this character stopped dimming?" produces service orientation
- "Someone reading it would know what this person does, who they do it for, and why they are the one doing it" produces a voiceprint

The identity work is named through immersion, not announcement: "you will not just have a new story — you will be living inside one" and "you have been working at the level of identity — the narrative layer where who you believe yourself to be is constructed." The person feels it because they have been doing it for seven chapters. The naming arrives as recognition, not instruction.

Story living enters in the opening: "a story lived long enough becomes indistinguishable from the person living it" and "you will be living inside one." The closing returns to it: "a story lived only for yourself is a story with a ceiling." The frame is that story is not something you tell. It is something you are. The authored narrative is the life you inhabit.
